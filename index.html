I'm a drone operator and content creator based in Norway, driven by a passion for visual storytelling and the pursuit of the perfect frame.

What started as a fascination with movement and perspective grew into a full creative practice — one built on the belief that great content doesn't just capture moments, it creates them.

Under the Apex North brand, I bring together aerial cinematography, FPV flying and handheld storytelling to create visuals that feel alive. Every project begins with a conversation and ends with something that genuinely resonates.

My approach is always collaborative. I don't just show up and fly — I get into your world, understand what you're trying to say, and build something that says it powerfully.

Whether it's a brand campaign, a social media series or a cinematic film project, the standard stays the same: quality, creativity and visual impact in every single frame.
